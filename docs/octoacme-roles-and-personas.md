## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Lead owns quality assurance strategy, test automation, and acceptance validation. They ensure all features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before reaching production.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain comprehensive test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Coordinate QA activities across sprints and coordinate with developers on test automation
- Identify quality gaps, regressions, and propose testing improvements
- Lead smoke testing and acceptance testing before production deployments
- Report on test coverage, critical bugs, and quality metrics in weekly syncs

### Goals
- Ensure all delivered features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards
- Maintain test coverage above agreed thresholds (e.g., >80% unit test coverage)
- Catch regressions and defects early to prevent production incidents
- Enable rapid, confident deployments through comprehensive test coverage

### Typical Communication
- Daily: standups with developers on testing progress and blockers
- Weekly: quality metrics and coverage reports in delivery sync
- Per-sprint: test plan reviews and acceptance criteria validation
- Per-release: smoke test coordination and go/no-go recommendations

### Key Interactions
- **With Developers:** Collaborates on test automation strategy, reviews PRs for test coverage, supports debugging of test failures
- **With Product Manager:** Validates acceptance criteria clarity, clarifies feature scope for testing, reviews success metrics
- **With Project Manager:** Reports quality blockers, escalates critical defects, supports release readiness assessments
- **With Stakeholders:** Communicates test coverage and quality metrics for executive visibility

### When this role is needed
- Essential in all phases: Planning (define test strategy), Execution (run tests), Release (validate readiness)
- Especially critical for: customer-facing features, integrations, regulatory compliance projects, high-reliability systems

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholder/Sponsor provides business context, executive approval, and escalation authority. They ensure projects align with business strategy and have necessary organizational support to succeed.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and strategic alignment for project scope
- Approve go/no-go decisions at key gates (Initiation, Release, significant scope changes)
- Support removal of organizational and cross-team blockers
- Participate in milestone reviews and provide feedback
- Escalate business-level risks and decisions requiring executive attention

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value aligned with strategy
- Remove organizational barriers that impede delivery
- Support and enable successful delivery of strategic initiatives
- Maintain stakeholder alignment and confidence

### Typical Communication
- Gate reviews: initiation kickoff, release approval, major scope changes
- Monthly/quarterly: strategic alignment and roadmap reviews
- Ad-hoc: escalation of business-blocking issues requiring executive decision
- Post-project: lessons learned and business impact assessment

### Key Interactions
- **With Project Manager:** Receives weekly status updates, reviews escalations, approves at gates
- **With Product Manager:** Provides business context and strategic priorities, reviews roadmap alignment
- **With Product Lead:** Aligns on strategic trade-offs and priority decisions
- **With all teams:** Provides visibility and support for organizational enablement

### When this role is needed
- Initiation phase: business case approval and resource commitment
- Planning: scope and timeline validation against business constraints
- Release phase: go/no-go approval for production deployment
- Throughout: escalation path for business-impacting decisions

## Product Lead

### Role Summary
Product Lead (senior product/technical leader) guides product strategy, makes prioritization trade-offs, and owns go/no-go decisions. They ensure coherent product strategy across multiple teams and initiatives.

### Responsibilities
- Guide product vision and strategic direction across all initiatives
- Make go/no-go decisions on features and experiments based on strategic fit
- Resolve product trade-offs between competing initiatives or teams
- Escalate and coordinate cross-team dependencies and conflicts
- Partner with PM and PdM on roadmap and release sequencing

### Goals
- Ensure coherent, strategic product direction
- Maximize impact of engineering and product efforts
- Reduce team-level friction through clear, decisive trade-off decisions
- Accelerate delivery by removing decision bottlenecks

### Typical Communication
- Weekly: alignment with PM/PdM on roadmap and prioritization
- Weekly: review of escalations and decisions from projects
- Monthly: strategic roadmap and product strategy reviews
- Per-project: review of trade-off recommendations from teams

### Key Interactions
- **With Product Manager:** Partners on product strategy, roadmap, and prioritization decisions
- **With Project Manager:** Reviews escalations and provides decision authority for trade-offs
- **With Sponsor:** Aligns on strategic priorities and business context
- **With Technical Lead:** Reviews technical feasibility and architecture decisions
- **With Developers:** Provides product strategy context and decision clarity

### When this role is needed
- Planning phase: prioritization of features and scope
- Execution: resolving scope trade-offs and competing priorities
- Release: go/no-go decisions for production deployment
- Ongoing: escalation path for product-level decisions

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Lead (senior engineer/architect) provides technical strategy, reviews architecture decisions, and guides the development team on technical approach. They ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with technical standards.

### Responsibilities
- Review and approve technical designs and architecture for major components
- Identify technical risks, performance concerns, and propose mitigations
- Mentor and coach developers on technical approach and best practices
- Guide decisions on technology choices, integrations, and system design
- Support estimation and feasibility assessment during planning

### Goals
- Build scalable, maintainable technical solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework through thoughtful architecture
- Accelerate developer velocity by providing clear technical patterns and decisions
- Maintain high code quality and system reliability

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews: technical design docs and architecture decisions
- Weekly: technical risk review in planning and execution syncs
- Daily: available for technical consultation with developers
- Per-sprint: code review of complex or risky changes

### Key Interactions
- **With Developers:** Guides technical approach, reviews designs, mentors on patterns and best practices
- **With Project Manager:** Reports technical risks, supports feasibility assessment, escalates technical blockers
- **With QA/Testing Lead:** Collaborates on test automation strategy and coverage requirements
- **With Product Lead:** Provides technical feasibility input on product decisions and trade-offs

### When this role is needed
- Planning phase: technical feasibility assessment and architecture design
- Execution: technical design reviews and mentoring
- Release: technical readiness validation
- Ongoing: technical decision authority and strategy guidance

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Master/Agile Coach facilitates Agile ceremonies, removes team impediments, and coaches the team on Agile practices. They enable the team to work effectively and continuously improve their processes.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, sprint reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help remove team blockers and impediments
- Coach team members on Agile practices and ceremonies (timeboxing, focus, participation)
- Track and monitor action items from retrospectives
- Support the team in maintaining sustainable pace and work-life balance

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and delivery efficiency
- Foster psychological safety, continuous learning, and improvement culture
- Keep ceremonies focused, timeboxed, and high-value
- Enable team self-organization and autonomy

### Typical Communication
- Daily: standups and blocker identification
- Weekly: sprint planning and retrospectives
- Ongoing: 1:1 coaching with team members on Agile practices
- Weekly: progress tracking and action item follow-up

### Key Interactions
- **With entire team:** Supports all members in ceremonies and continuous improvement
- **With Project Manager:** Works with PM on sprint planning and capacity management
- **With Developers:** Removes impediments, supports focus and sustainable pace
- **With all roles:** Creates psychological safety and encourages participation

### When this role is needed
- Essential in all phases for teams practicing Agile/Scrum
- Sprint planning and execution: daily facilitation
- Retrospectives: after each sprint, release, or significant milestone
- Ongoing: continuous improvement and team coaching

## Role Interaction Matrix

| Phase | Developers | PdM | PM | QA Lead | Tech Lead | Sponsor | Product Lead | Scrum Master |
|-------|-----------|-----|----|---------|-----------|---------|--------------| -------------|
| **Initiation** | Advisory | Owner | Owner | Advisory | Advisory | Approver | Approver | Observer |
| **Planning** | Active | Active | Owner | Owner | Owner | Advisory | Advisor | Facilitator |
| **Execution** | Owner | Active | Active | Active | Advisory | Observer | Advisory | Facilitator |
| **Release** | Active | Active | Owner | Owner | Advisory | Approver | Approver | Support |
| **Retrospective** | Active | Active | Active | Active | Active | Observer | Observer | Facilitator |

**Legend:**
- **Owner:** drives the activity and makes decisions
- **Active:** participates and contributes significantly
- **Advisor:** provides input and guidance
- **Approver:** must approve before proceeding
- **Facilitator:** runs the ceremony or process
- **Advisory:** consulted as needed
- **Observer:** attends for visibility
