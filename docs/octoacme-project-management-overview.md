## Core Roles

OctoAcme projects engage 8 key personas, depending on project scope and complexity:

**Leadership & Strategy:**
- **Product Lead:** Guides product strategy, makes go/no-go decisions on features
- **Sponsor/Stakeholder:** Provides business context and executive approval

**Product & Planning:**
- **Product Manager (PdM):** Defines outcomes, prioritizes backlog, measures success
- **Project Manager (PM):** Coordinates delivery, manages timeline and risks

**Execution & Quality:**
- **Developers:** Implement features and maintain code quality
- **Technical Lead:** Guides architecture and technical approach
- **QA/Testing Lead:** Ensures quality and validates acceptance criteria
- **Scrum Master/Agile Coach:** Facilitates ceremonies and enables team effectiveness
